Level 2 · Candlesticks

A candlestick tells you 4 things.

Where price started. Where it ended. How high it got. How low it went. That's all a candlestick is — a snapshot of price during a time period.

Two Types of Candles

The color tells you which way price moved.

Green — price went UP

The open is at the bottom of the body. The close is at the top. Price started low and ended higher.

Red — price went DOWN

The open is at the top of the body. The close is at the bottom. Price started high and ended lower.

Each candle = one time period

On a daily chart, each candle is one day. On a 1-hour chart, each candle is one hour. The candles below show 7 days of price action — each one is a full trading day.

The thin lines above and below the colored body are called wicks. The top wick = the High. The bottom wick = the Low. The body in the middle = where price opened and closed.
